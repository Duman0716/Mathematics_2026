### Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

**Given:**
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -2 & 3 \\ 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

1. **Sizes of matrices:**
   - $A$ has $2$ rows and $3$ columns, so its size is **$2 \times 3$**.
   - $B$ has $3$ rows and $2$ columns, so its size is **$3 \times 2$**.

2. **Reading off entries:**
   - $a_{12} = -1$ (row 1, column 2 of $A$)
   - $a_{23} = 5$ (row 2, column 3 of $A$)
   - $b_{21} = -2$ (row 2, column 1 of $B$)
   - $b_{32} = 1$ (row 3, column 2 of $B$)

3. **Rows and columns as vectors:**
   - Second row of $A$: $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$
   - First column of $B$: $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$

> **Property / Method:** Matrix dimensions are denoted as $\text{rows} \times \text{columns}$. Indices $a_{ij}$ refer to row $i$ and column $j$.
